Mean residual as a function of each covariate. Each covariate is binned into quantiles. Shaded bands are 95% CIs from cluster bootstrap (resampling stations, 1000 iterations).

Temperature × wind interaction

Same PDP for station temperature, but stratified by wind speed tercile. The fan-out at high temperatures shows that low wind amplifies ERA5’s warm bias.
